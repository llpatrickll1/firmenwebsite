Temperature
Control

High-temperature processes need sophisticated and highly reliable control — especially when a process runs for months, as in crystal growth or high-tech glass and ceramics.

MODCON can control up to 30 independent heat zones. MODVIS provides process visualization and supervision. For smaller applications, PR2 provides two-zone control, while MPC20 and MPC32 handle high-precision heater power control.
